Know exactly what you earn each day

Profitwise turns your orders, costs, fees, VAT and ad spend into one clear daily profit figure, and keeps a record of every day.

Go to Dashboard Open calculator

How it works

  1. Set your costs

    Enter revenue per order, COGS, fees, commissions, VAT and shipping once in the calculator settings.

  2. Enter the day

    Add the number of orders and your ad spend. Results update as you type.

  3. Save it

    Pick a date and save. The day appears in your calendar with its net profit and margin.

What you see

Revenue
Total sales for the day.
Gross profit
Profit before advertising costs.
Net profit
What is left after all costs, fees, VAT and ad spend.
Profit margin
Net profit as a percentage of revenue.
ROAS
Revenue earned for every euro spent on ads.
